HERMEiAS acquires Cracked.Social, will relaunch events in Bangalore and Delhi

HERMEiAS has acquired Cracked.Social, the founder and builder community behind three events since November 2024. Following a co-founder split, the brand is relaunching under HERMEiAS with hackathons and community events planned across Bangalore and Delhi.
